# HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Interactive Dashboard & Cultural Audit using MS Excel/Power BI)

## Project Objective
The objective of this project was to provide a comprehensive analysis of a workforce consisting of 1,233 employees to understand departmental health, employee satisfaction, and financial distribution. By visualizing a $10M monthly payroll, the project aims to help HR leadership identify retention risks and optimize talent management strategies for 2026.

## Dataset used
- Dataset: <a href="https://github.com/IbrahimEmarah/hr-workforce-analytics-dashboard/blob/main/Human%20Resources%20Dashboard%20Project%20Datasheets.xlsx">Workforce Census & Employee Satisfaction Survey Data.</a>
- Scope: 1,233 Active Employees across 3 Departments (Sales, R&D, and HR).

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the total monthly payroll, and how is it distributed across different job roles?
- What is the demographic breakdown of the company by Age Band and Education Field?
- Which job roles (e.g., Sales Executive, Research Scientist) represent the highest financial investment?
- Satisfaction Audit: What are the median scores for Environment, Relationship, and Job Satisfaction?
- How does the Performance Rating correlate with different age demographics?
- Which department has the highest concentration of "Life Sciences" vs. "Medical" graduates?

## Process
- Data Harmonization: Cleaned and structured employee records to ensure age bands (Under 25 to Over 55) and education fields were standardized for accurate filtering.
- Metric Engineering: Calculated median satisfaction scores using 1-4 scales to provide a realistic "Pulse" of the company culture beyond simple averages.
- UI/UX Design: Developed two distinct interface versions (High-Contrast Dark Theme and Professional Light Theme) to demonstrate versatility in dashboard presentation.
- Dynamic Integration: Used Slicers to allow HR managers to filter the entire dashboard by "Age Band," immediately updating satisfaction gauges and income charts.

## Project Insight
- The Talent Backbone: 41.2% (606 employees) of the workforce are Life Sciences graduates, showing a strong, specialized technical foundation in the company.
- Retention Risk: While Performance Ratings are high (3.15/4), Relationship and Environment Satisfaction scores are lower (2.71 and 2.72), indicating a potential risk for turnover due to internal culture.
- Key Demographic: The 25–34 age group is the largest and most active segment, making them the primary target for long-term career development programs.
- Investment Concentration: Sales Executives and Research Scientists command the highest monthly rates, aligning with the company's focus on revenue and innovation.

## Final Conclusion
To improve employee retention and organizational health, HR should launch an internal "Culture Initiative" specifically targeting relationship satisfaction, as this is the lowest-scoring metric. Furthermore, since the 25–34 age demographic is the largest, creating clear "Promotion Pathways" for this group will ensure the $10M monthly talent investment continues to yield high performance and loyalty.
